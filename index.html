Small electron polaron in rutile TiO2

DFT+U (VASP) study of an excess electron trapped on a single Ti site of rutile TiO2: site-controlled trapping, the polaron formation energy (EPOL = −0.163 eV, stable), and the in-gap polaron level 0.92 eV below the conduction band.
